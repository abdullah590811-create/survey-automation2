How to get your exact Google Form Link

Google Forms needs the hidden internal numbers (entry.XXXXX) to actually register responses in the Google Sheet. Here is the easiest 30-second method to link them:

  1. Open your Google Form in edit mode.
  2. Click the three dots (⋮) at the top right of Google Forms.
  3. Click "Get pre-filled link" (الحصول على الرابط الذي تم ملؤه مسبقًا).
  4. Choose any random answer for the questions, then scroll to the bottom and click "Get link" (الحصول على الرابط).
  5. Click "Copy link".
  6. Paste that copied link into Step 1 on this dashboard and tap Link & Sync Survey!
